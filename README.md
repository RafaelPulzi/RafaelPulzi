### What I work on

Most of my work is batch data pipelines: incremental ingestion from REST APIs and relational sources, transformation in PySpark and SQL, bronze, silver and gold layering, data quality checks, and scheduled orchestration.

I also have a research project in computer vision for public services. It detects waste with YOLO and OpenCV and feeds a georeferenced route prioritization system. The project has a patent pending.

The rest of what is here is automation. Local workflows that cut out repetitive manual steps, multi-API integrations, report generation, ESC/POS thermal printing, and tooling for Obsidian vaults.
